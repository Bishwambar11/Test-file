The 2026 Mercedes-Benz G-Class continues its iconic, boxy design with modern updates like LED lighting, while offering new powertrain options, including a mild-hybrid inline-6 (G 550), a powerful V8 (AMG G 63), and a new all-electric G 580 with four motors. Key features include a luxurious interior with dual 12.3-inch screens, advanced MBUX infotainment, and legendary off-road capability with three locking differentials, though the EV version has limited range. Powertrains G 550: 3.0L inline-6 turbo with mild-hybrid assist (443 hp). AMG G 63: Handcrafted 4.0L V8 biturbo with hybrid assist (577 hp). G 580 (Electric): Four electric motors (579 hp, 859 lb-ft torque). Key Features Off-Road: Three locking differentials, high ground clearance, and excellent approach/departure angles. Interior: Nappa leather, open-pore wood, dual 12.3-inch MBUX screens, and available massage seats. Technology: MBUX with voice control, off-road cockpit displays, and advanced driver-assistance systems like adaptive cruise control and blind-spot monitoring. Design: Retains classic G-Wagen look with modern touches like new colors and optional illuminated logos. Performance & Range G 550: 0-60 mph in 5.3 seconds, 18 MPG combined. AMG G 63: 0-60 mph in 4.2 seconds, 15 MPG combined. G 580 (Electric): 0-60 mph in 4.6 seconds, but limited to 239 miles of range. Cargo & Practicality Cargo space is similar to a compact crossover, with the EV losing a small amount due to the battery. Towing capacity is up to 7,000 lbs.
